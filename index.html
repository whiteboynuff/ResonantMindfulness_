The method

It fits in one sentence.

Lengthen the out-breath. Leave the in-breath alone and let it come when it comes. Your nervous system reads a long exhale as safety, and it reads it before you have finished thinking about whether any of this works.

No thought has to leave. Nothing gets pushed away, argued with, or replaced with a better thought. Every book in the series walks back to the same move from a different angle, in kitchen table language, with the reasons laid out so you can check them in your own body instead of taking my word.

The source material is Zen, Advaita Vedanta, Dzogchen and Taoism. The vocabulary that usually keeps people out has been stripped from all of it.
